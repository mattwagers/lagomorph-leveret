The word maze

In this experiment you'll read English sentences one word at a time. The catch is that you build each sentence yourself: at every step you see two words, side by side. Only one of them continues the sentence as natural English. Your job is to pick it.

Rest your fingers on the keyboard: left index finger on e, right index finger on i.

  • Press e to choose the word on the left.
  • Press i to choose the word on the right.

Every sentence starts the same way: The on one side and x-x-x on the other. Here is what the first two steps of a sentence might look like:

The
e
x-x-x
i

→ press e for "The". Then:

about
e
baker
i

→ press i: "The baker" is English; "The about" is not.

The correct word switches sides at random, so you'll need to read both words each time. The wrong word is always a real word, but one that doesn't fit at that point in the sentence.

Confirm your understanding
1. Which key chooses the word on the right?
2. Which word should you choose?
