Speed and mistakes

Go as quickly as you can while still reading for meaning. Try to understand each sentence as you build it, the way you would if you were reading normally. Many of the sentences are fairly long, and some take a moment to work out. That's expected.

If you choose the wrong word, you'll see Incorrect! in red. After a short pause the message changes to Please try again., and you choose again. Once you pick the right word, the sentence goes on. Keys pressed during the pause are ignored, so guessing quickly doesn't save time.

Everyone makes occasional mistakes, and sometimes the wrong word may seem possible too. Don't worry about it; just pick the other word and keep going. However, we can tell when someone presses keys without reading (their accuracy is close to chance), and we can't use that data.

Between sentences you'll see a screen asking you to press the space bar when you're ready for the next one, so you can pause there if you need to. Please don't pause in the middle of a sentence.
