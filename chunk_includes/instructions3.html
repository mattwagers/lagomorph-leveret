Instructions (3/3)

Questions, breaks, and setup

After each sentence you'll get a yes/no question about what it said. Use the same two keys:

  • e = Yes (shown on the left)
  • i = No (shown on the right)

The questions are there to help you keep track of what you're reading. Answer from the sentence you just read, not from what seems likely in general.

There will be a short practice session first, with feedback on your answers. After that, the main part has about 100 sentences, with breaks along the way.

Setup. You need a physical keyboard (this study can't be done on a phone or tablet). Please maximize your browser window, close other tabs, and silence your phone and notifications for the next 35 minutes.
